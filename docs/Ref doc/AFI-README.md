# 🌡 AFI / SARS-CoV-2 Spatial Distribution — Monrovia, Liberia (December 2021)

---

## Overview

This project maps the **spatial distribution of Acute Febrile Illness (AFI) patients** who tested positive for **SARS-CoV-2** across communities in **Greater Monrovia, Liberia**.

The analysis was conducted to support **COVID-19 surveillance and response efforts** during a critical phase of the pandemic.

---

## Project Context

This work was developed under the **Acute Febrile Illness (AFI) Study** as part of Liberia’s national COVID-19 surveillance program.

The objective was to support **epidemiological understanding of SARS-CoV-2 circulation** in densely populated urban communities and inform **targeted public-health interventions**.

## Analytical Overview

A high-resolution epidemiological map was produced to visualize AFI patients who tested positive for SARS-CoV-2 using:

- RT-PCR
- Serological methods (IgG, IgM)

The map highlights spatial clustering and exposure patterns in high-density urban communities, including:

- West Point
- Clara Town
- Logan Town
- Central Monrovia

---

## Key Features & Methodology

### Multi-Class Diagnostic Visualization

- Implemented multi-variable thematic symbology using color-coded point markers
- Visualized **seven diagnostic result combinations**, including:
  - RT-PCR positive
  - IgG reactive
  - IgM reactive
  - Combined IgM/IgG reactivity
  - PCR and serology combinations

---

### Point Density & Case Burden Representation

- Labeled map symbols by number of cases per location
- Enabled rapid identification of spatial clustering and hotspots
- Highlighted areas of intensified transmission risk

---

### Spatial Context & Insets

- Included inset maps of Liberia and Greater Monrovia
- Overlaid administrative boundaries and community zones
- Provided spatial context for national and international stakeholders

---

### Cartographic Design & Map Elements

- Detailed diagnostic legend for analytical clarity
- North arrow, scale bar, and coordinate grid
- High-resolution layout optimized for reporting and presentations

---

## GIS Tasks Performed

- Cleaned, processed, and validated patient location and laboratory result data
- Performed spatial joins to associate cases with community zones
- Designed multi-class symbology to encode diagnostic complexity
- Produced publication-quality maps for epidemiological reporting

---

## Impact & Use

This work enabled epidemiologists and public-health decision-makers to:

- Identify COVID-19 exposure patterns in high-density urban settings
- Prioritize surveillance, testing, and health messaging
- Support data-driven response strategies during a critical phase of Liberia’s COVID-19 response

The outputs contributed to **national and international situational awareness efforts**.
