### Lassa Fever GeoAI Forecasting — Liberia (2016–2026)
**Role:** Lead Analyst  
**Tools:** Python, GeoPandas, XGBoost, Prophet, ARIMA  

Developed a reproducible spatio-temporal forecasting pipeline combining national
time-series models with county-level GeoAI prediction to support outbreak preparedness
and early-warning surveillance.

**Impact**
- Enabled predictive risk mapping through 2026
- Produced manuscript-ready figures for public health stakeholders

🔗 **GitHub Repository:**  
https://tinyurl.com/29y28n24

### Intrusion Detection with Big Data Analytics (UNSW-NB15)
**Role:** Big Data & Security Analyst  
**Tools:** Hive, PySpark, Spark MLlib  

Designed and evaluated a distributed machine learning pipeline on 2.54M network records
to detect cyberattacks using Logistic Regression and Random Forest classifiers.

**Impact**
- Achieved AUC up to 0.987
- Demonstrated scalable, SOC-aligned analytics suitable for SIEM/SOC workflows

🔗 **GitHub Repository:**  
https://tinyurl.com/bddzpbkw

---

### NC Emergency Management — Building Exposure Analysis
**Role:** GIS & Data Engineer  
**Tools:** GeoPandas, ArcGIS Pro  

Designed a Python-based workflow to clean, validate, and analyze building footprints
for hazard exposure modeling (flood, surge, wildfire) to support emergency preparedness
and mitigation planning.

**Impact**
- Supports data-driven emergency planning and risk mitigation
- Enables reproducible, auditable hazard exposure assessments
