# 🧳 Cross-Border Ebola & Marburg Risk Mapping

## Overview
Risk modeling along Liberia’s borders with Guinea, Sierra Leone, and Côte d’Ivoire.

Uses:
- Mobility corridor analysis  
- Ecological suitability factors  
- Vulnerability scoring  

## Outputs
- Border risk maps  
- Priority surveillance zones
